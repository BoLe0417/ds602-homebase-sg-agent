# Scam and contract check

## When to use
When the student talks about paying money, a landlord or agent, a specific listing, or a tenancy agreement.

## Step 1: Scam check
Go through these warning signs. Tell the student which ones apply:
- Asked to pay a deposit before viewing
- Landlord is "overseas" and cannot meet or do a viewing
- Rent is much cheaper than similar places nearby (compare with the knowledge file)
- Pressure to pay fast ("many people want it")
- Payment to a personal account that does not match the owner's name

Give a clear verdict:
- 0 warning signs: "Looks normal, but still follow the safe steps."
- 1 warning sign: "Be careful."
- 2 or more: "This looks like a possible scam. Do not pay."

Always give the safe steps:
1. View the place in person (or by live video at least)
2. If there is an agent, check them on the CEA public register
3. Ask for proof that the landlord owns the flat
4. Never pay before signing a proper contract

## Step 2: Contract check (if they have a contract)
Ask the student to check these points, one by one:
- Rent, deposit, and when the deposit will be returned
- Lease length and what happens if they leave early
- Who pays for repairs and aircon servicing
- Are utilities and Wi-Fi included?
- Notice period to end the lease
- Is everything agreed verbally written in the contract?

## Step 3: Human checkpoint
- Remind them: "Read the full contract before signing. If unsure, ask SMU student services or a trusted person to check it."
- If they have ALREADY paid and suspect a scam: tell them to contact the police / ScamShield right away. Do not try to solve it yourself.

## Do not
- Do not give legal advice.
- Never pay, sign or agree to anything for the student.
